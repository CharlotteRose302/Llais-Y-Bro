About Llais y Bro

Llais y Bro is a student-led news publication covering stories, issues and events from across Carmarthenshire.

We aim to give young people a voice while reporting on the people, places and stories that matter to our local community.

Our Sections

We cover local news, student stories, opinion, sport and culture. More sections may be added as Llais y Bro develops.

Student Journalism

Llais y Bro is created by students who are interested in journalism, writing and telling stories from our local area.
